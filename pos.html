How It Works

From Login to Receipt — In Seconds

The POS system guides the cashier through a smooth, fast workflow designed for real restaurant and market environments.

01
Login & Open Shift
The cashier logs in with their credentials. A shift opens automatically, recording the start time, opening balance, and cashier identity. Every sale links to that shift.
02
Browse & Add Products
Products are displayed as a visual grid with photos and IQD prices. Tap a product to add it to the cart. Category tabs (Meat, Coffee, Bread, Drinks) filter the grid instantly.
03
Manage the Cart
The left panel shows the live cart with quantity, unit price, discount, and line total. Items can be edited, removed, or have notes added. Assign the order to a customer account.
04
Payment & Change
Smart numpad and quick-select IQD banknotes (1K, 5K, 10K, 25K, 50K) make cash entry instant. Supports Cash and Card. USD rate shown live. Change calculated automatically.
05
Print Receipt
One click prints a professional thermal receipt with business name, cashier, date, all items, taxes, total, amount paid, and change. WhatsApp sharing is also supported.
06
Close Shift & Z-Report
At end of day, the cashier closes the shift. The system auto-generates a Z-Report: total sales, cash in drawer, card sales, number of receipts, and a Cash Handover document.

Shift Management

Cash Drawer & X/Z Reports

Full shift tracking so the owner always knows what cash is in the drawer, who sold what, and when every shift opened and closed.

Key Features

Built-In Capabilities

Every feature designed for the real workflow of a Kurdish restaurant or market.

Multi-Language
Full Kurdish (Sorani), Kurdish (Badini), Arabic, and English. RTL/LTR auto-switches. Even the receipt prints in the selected language.
IQD + USD Dual Currency
All prices in Iraqi Dinar. Live USD exchange rate shown next to every total. Cashier sees both currencies simultaneously.
Thermal Receipt Printing
Prints to any 58mm or 80mm thermal printer. Auto-print on checkout, or preview first. WhatsApp share option available.
Beautiful Dark UI
Premium glassmorphism dark interface. Comfortable for all-day use in any lighting. Light mode also available via toggle.
License & Auth System
Hardware-locked license key system. Each business gets a unique license. Role-based login for owners and cashiers.
Barcode Scanner Support
Plug in any USB barcode scanner. Generates and prints barcodes for products that don't have one.
Excel Export
Export daily reports, shift summaries, and cash handover documents directly to .xlsx Excel files.
USB Backup & Restore
Back up the entire database to a USB drive with one click. Restore from backup in seconds. Never lose data.
Offline Desktop App
Works completely offline as a Windows desktop app (Electron). No internet required. All data stays local.
